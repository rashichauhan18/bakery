Our Heritage

Born in Colaba,
Loved Across the Country

Theobroma began its journey in 2004 as a humble family-run patisserie in the iconic lanes of Colaba, Mumbai. Founded by Kainaz Messman Harchandrai with a simple dream: to bring classic, wholesome, honestly-priced baked goods to everyone.

Named after the Greek botanical name for cacao — *Theobroma*, meaning "Food of the Gods" — we treat every bake as an offering of delight, staying uncompromising on real butter, pure cocoa, and traditional craftsmanship.
